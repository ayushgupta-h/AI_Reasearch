Not everyone makes it in.

Most tools hand you an answer and hope you don't look too closely. Quest was built for the ones who do — the people who read the footnotes, who check the citation, who keep asking why. Five agents take your question apart, chase it across the web, and argue with each other until whatever survives can be defended out loud.

Capabilities

Built like a research desk, not a chat window.

Three habits separate a desk from a conversation: it looks in many places, it doubts its own drafting, and it keeps a human in the loop.

01

Multi-source search

Web, news, academic, and internal reports queried in parallel — then deduplicated and ranked before a word is written.

02

Self-critique pass

A dedicated agent attacks every draft for weak claims, stale sources, and unsupported numbers before you ever see it.

03

Human review

Nothing ships on its own. You approve, annotate, or rerun any section — the desk works for you, not instead of you.
